Group photographs for the album at the top of the People page.
They are shown whole (not cropped), at a fixed height, in the order group-01, group-02, ...
To add a photo: save it here (JPEG, about 2000 px wide, under 500 KB) and add a <figure> to the album in dmmg/people.html
with its width and height. Captions go in the empty <figcaption> under each photo.
