About

I lead the Deformation Mechanisms and Modeling Group (DMMG), which studies the links between microstructure and the mechanical response of structural materials in extreme operating environments. We combine mechanical testing at high strain rates and high temperatures, microstructural characterization (XRD, SEM, TEM) and multi-scale modeling (DFT, MD, MC, phase-field, FEM). At a fundamental level the work is about plastic deformation mechanisms, which are affected by the interplay between composition, phase stability, microstructure and crystal defects.

I teach several postgraduate and undergraduate courses, focusing on mechanical properties, structure and crystal defects.
