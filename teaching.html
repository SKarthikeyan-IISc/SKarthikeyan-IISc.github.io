Teaching

I teach postgraduate and undergraduate courses at IISc, and short courses for industry and other institutions. I try to make a topic accessible without losing depth, and to bring out the physics so that students do not get lost in the mathematics.

My proudest moment was when I received the IISc Excellence in Teaching Award in 2024.

Teaching tools

Animations and simulations I use in class, which run in any web browser: dislocations, phase diagrams and deformation mechanisms.
